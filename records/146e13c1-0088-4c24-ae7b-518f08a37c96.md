Collins submitted the Hungarian-algorithm analogy as the first branch. After a flow rerouting, the first new tie determines a safe price jump. Bounded margin classes and bounded independent cycle products give a polynomial subclass. In general the stated rule can meet many internal events even at Hall deficit one.
