Theorem 1.2 of Ghorbani, Lopes and Mnich supplies a block solver for least fixed points of lattice self-maps with unit increments. The market proof starts with A4f2's signed real contraction, rounds its transfers while preserving unit increments, and shows that conditional elimination preserves the map class. A4d gives exact rational and support recovery, A4c selects minimum prices through enlarged supplies, and L4b transfers the bound to exact game equilibrium. The result is $2^{O(\sqrt d\log(2+L))}$ bit time for $d$ buyer and good coordinates and binary input length $L$. The cube-root runtime of the source concerns its switch-graph problem. [Market construction and full bit bound](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/cb3901dc26b8a1b9ad162f4a4808448a1f8c0717/notes/A4m-discrete-contraction.md) · [Ghorbani, Lopes and Mnich, Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28241v1#S1.Thmtheorem1.2)

Run `python3 -B experiments/a4m_lattice_replay.py`. The replay checks 3,121 lattice points, 10,048 one-unit increments, and 8,464 increments of conditional maps. It also reproduces a case where rounding each coordinate separately changes two output coordinates.
