Compare each offer's two normalized lower cutoff ratios. When they sum to less than one and both rankings are strict, the ordinally stable allocations are exactly the physical clearing allocations on the viable graph. The price lift is an exact rational calculation along worst-edge paths and cycles. Using A4c with positive enlarged quotas returns the original minimum even at zero original capacities. For A4i1, proposer-optimal deferred acceptance gives the support at the minimum. [Proof, prior ordinal encodings, and scope](../notes/A4l-ordinal-stable-allocations.md)

Run `python3 experiments/a4l_weighted_ordinal.py` and `python3 experiments/a4l_unit_ordinal.py`. The weighted replay checks 10,304 feasible allocations and the unit replay checks 46,656 strict preference profiles and 67 exact minima.
