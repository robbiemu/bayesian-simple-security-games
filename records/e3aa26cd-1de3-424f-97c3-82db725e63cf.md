A4a supplies the frozen assignment map and the repair that keeps every observed demand tie. By bounding changes along alternating paths, this contribution gives a single rational LP that certifies sup-norm nonexpansiveness of the frozen map. OpenAI's November 24 manuscript supplies the polynomial-time oracle for small residuals of such maps. The new factor certificate and the transfer from those residuals to exact least prices are the market contribution. The manuscript's approximation theorem is used as stated. [Proof and separation from the column certificate](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/cb3901dc26b8a1b9ad162f4a4808448a1f8c0717/notes/A4m-path-sensitive-freezing.md) · [OpenAI, Approximation in max norm](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Strongly-polynomial-linear-programming-November-24-2026/build/contractions.tex#L1-L38)

The A4f2 map and the A4h region search provide the second context for testing transfer. A single local A4f2 evaluation can have the same affine formula in two markets whose solution regions and required extremal signs differ. The exact cut-coordinate lift clarifies why a direct representation can have exponential size. [Extremal-price interface and exact cut lift](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/cb3901dc26b8a1b9ad162f4a4808448a1f8c0717/notes/A4m-extremal-price-interface.md)
