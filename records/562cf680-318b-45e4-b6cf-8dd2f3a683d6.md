The degree-three normal form reduces the open A4i1 task. A4i1b provides an independent exact solver for the test instances. Alternating paths of private goods and buyers route each original trade through a dedicated edge. Taking the maximum price along each good path decodes the original minimum. [Construction](../notes/A4j-unit-degree-three.md)

Run `python3 experiments/a4j_degree_three_replay.py`. It tests 80 seeded rational markets, including omitted offers and original goods that remain at reserve.
