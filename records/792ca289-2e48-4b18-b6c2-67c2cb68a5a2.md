The [general reverse reduction](./f4f5b6ed-bad7-4af7-bb54-4175c6f95eed.md) pins one score by a padding target. In the unit case, splitting fixed padding attack among several covered targets pins the needed fractional score with uniform priors and unit gains. In the other direction, A1 bounds the threshold list by Hall ratios, A4c supplies the capacity perturbation, and L4b recovers the supplied budget from extremal and reserve queries. Every query has a common capacity after normalizing the row masses to one. Its comparisons with fractions $a/b$ for $0\le a\le k$ and $1\le b\le n$ suffice to replace it by a small exact copy count. [Unit-market equivalence](../notes/A4i-market-to-game.md#jointly-uniform-games-and-unit-markets) · [A1 threshold interval](../notes/A1-threshold-transport.md#1-fixed-coverage-gives-an-exact-flow-test) · [A4c perturbation](../notes/A4c-any-clearing-equilibrium.md#one-call-at-enlarged-capacities) · [L4b threshold and budget recovery](../notes/L4-newton-and-continuation.md#exact-threshold-and-budget-recovery-with-a-clearing-oracle)

**Exact checks.** For each $1\le k\le8$ and $1\le n\le7$, form the sorted distinct fractions $a/b$. Test every positive breakpoint, every adjacent midpoint, and $k+1/2$. The representative agrees on every strict comparison and equality and satisfies the stated numerator and denominator bounds. For example, $7/10$ with three buyers and two goods is represented by $2/3$, giving nine unit buyers and four unit goods.

For one unit good and unit buyers with offers $5-2p$ and $4-p$, the enlarged capacity is $3/2$ and the minimum is $5/2$. The reverse game has five uniformly likely types, two padding targets at coverage $1/4$ each, the old target at coverage $1/2$, and two outside targets at zero. The old target and each padding target receive mass $3/10$, and each outside target receives $1/20$. The shared threshold is $3/10=3/(5\cdot2)$, on the finite Hall list, and the coverages sum to the single resource. [Open unit-market search](../investigation/A4i1.md)
