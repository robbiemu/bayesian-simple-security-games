The proxy maps the original incidence graph to the goods-interaction graph. A4h1's one-good block solver applies there, and A4b gives the local solves for the bounded vertex-cover and few-margin extension. L4b then supplies the shared threshold and budget recovery for the game. A1 handles weak signs on a viability-preserving perturbation. This lifts the shared boundary ideas in B3d and A4h9 to mixed articulation trees without requiring certified ties. The exact lift check used Python's rational arithmetic and seed 96391, with one to six goods and buyers. It generated a clearing certificate from chosen rational prices, row utilities, and supported trades. The proof gives an equation-by-equation reconstruction for any such certificate.

Run `python3 experiments/market_checks/a4h12_buyer_proxy_lift.py` from the repository root. The displayed proxy fixture and 240 seeded clearing witnesses lift and recover exactly, including the outside flow. This version attaches the exact replay and its input data after the [PR review](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/pull/7#issuecomment-7086828698).
