Shiva Kintali's November 24, 2026 preprint gives the equilibrium algorithm, using Baïou and Balinski's ordinal transportation problem. The Lean solver follows the grouped quota sweep and weighted quote search. Its exact recovery augments the fixed witness and projects a relaxed contact onto integer pattern equations. [Kintali's preprint at the pinned revision](https://github.com/shivakintali/shivakintali.github.io/blob/b28db0428e4cdcef7e9048a386b05d942d1eb57e/papers/SecurityGames-short.pdf) · [Paper-to-Lean correspondence](../lean/Kintali/README.md)

Lean proves that the executable algorithm returns an exact equilibrium for every valid rational game and that the compact encoding agrees with the pinned public definitions. The assembled solver has polynomial work in the project’s binary word-cost model. Lean also verifies recovery of a defender lottery with at most one pure defense per target and bounds the work of constructing it. The public polynomial-time statement still requires a single finite machine with polynomially bounded executions. [Exact solver](../lean/Kintali/Solver.lean) · [Measured binary solver](../lean/Kintali/InputOutput/MeasuredSolver.lean) · [Lottery work](../lean/Kintali/InputOutput/LotteryWork.lean) · [Machine refinement](../lean/Kintali/Machine/README.md)

From `lean/`, run `lake --wfail build Kintali`, `lake env lean Replay/KintaliAxioms.lean`, and `lake env lean Replay/KintaliSearch.lean`. The compatibility replay uses the public Formal Conjectures revision `9bd16c570aed942e00ae000c7522dd42ebbb25eb`. [Compatibility replay](../lean/Kintali/InputOutput/check_upstream.py)

The Python implementation ports the checked solver’s grouped sweep, compressed quote search, and exact repair. The cross-language replay checks the two outputs against exact equilibrium conditions. [Python solver and replay](../lean/Kintali/README.md#python-implementation)
