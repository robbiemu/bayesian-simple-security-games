For a designated starting state and a binary rational threshold, the source problem asks whether the optimal probability of reaching Win is at least the threshold. The target asks whether the lower limiting average is at least zero in a deterministic two-player graph with binary integer weights.

The question grew out of the investigation of feedback in affine-price gadgets. Its success criterion concerns the game models themselves. [October 2026 seminar](https://mimuw.edu.pl/en/seminars/talk/quasipolynomial-algorithms-for-mean-payoff-and-simple-stochastic-games/)
