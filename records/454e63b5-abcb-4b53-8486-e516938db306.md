Collins supplied the dominance analogy as a fallback after the alternating-forest and forced-tie passes. At given boundary prices and shared-buyer utilities, one conditional internal price vector preserves every concrete clearing completion when its joint feasible flows are retained. B3d uses a related switching idea for a game separator. The general difficulty is finding the boundary quotes efficiently.
