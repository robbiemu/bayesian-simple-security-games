This [review follow-up](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/pull/9#issuecomment-7089815875) checks the exclusion LP in the endpoint support proof. A fractional comparator gives optimum \(-1/2\) for one forced edge although each integral comparator has a nonnegative worst gap. The excluded offer is tight at the minimum. Deleting it from the cover would lower the supporting price from \(7/4\) to \(5/3\). A second fixture gives optimum zero and a minimum-price matching that uses the tested edge.

Run `python3 -B experiments/a4j_exclusion_lp_replay.py`.
