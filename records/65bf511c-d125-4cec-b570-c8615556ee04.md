The proof constructs one deterministic mean-payoff game whose zero-threshold answer agrees with the supplied stochastic reachability threshold. It permits nonabsorbing source plays and equality at the threshold. A uniform rational violation bound makes the published dyadic averaging recurrence sufficient for exact feasibility of the whole cyclic system. The note credits the previous recurrence and one-sided encoding. [Full proof at the evidence commit](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/d2ec644e20aad6447a29dc83646ea8580688728a/notes/H4-ssg-to-mean-payoff.md) · [Prior work](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/d2ec644e20aad6447a29dc83646ea8580688728a/notes/H4-averaging-prior-art.md) · [Search and stopping route](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/d2ec644e20aad6447a29dc83646ea8580688728a/notes/H4-deterministic-game-reductions.md) · [Stochastic mean-payoff extension](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/blob/d2ec644e20aad6447a29dc83646ea8580688728a/notes/H4-stochastic-mean-payoff.md)

```sh
python3 experiments/h4_ssg_to_mpg_replay.py --route direct --random 0 --systematic 2 --require-smt
```
