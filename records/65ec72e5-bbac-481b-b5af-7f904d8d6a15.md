B1 asked how to combine progress across disconnected components when one component determines the common threshold. Consider strictly positive losses and gains, a fixed seed order, and the exact implementation in L2. In one phase with fixed open boundary, count successes by the viable-choice component containing the chosen seed.

At such a success the reachable targets of that seed form a local maximizing Hall set. Its threshold is determined by the local demand signature, the local boundary, and the fixed input masses and gains. If the same local signature recurred at a later success seeded in this component during the same phase, it would give the same threshold. An intervening reset would have strictly lowered it. With the threshold and margins fixed, steps seeded in another component preserve the local demand graph. L2’s seed, cost and distance argument can then be applied to the local successes. [Hall ratio and non-repetition](../notes/L2-exact-arithmetic-audit.md#non-repetition-with-exact-flow-ties)

This suggests adding the local signature bounds in each phase. A bounded maximum local type count, or a bounded maximum local rank of the normalized payoff comparisons after B1’s sign refinement, would yield a polynomial bound for this strict-game continuation. An independent review should check the localization under flow ties, the phase and sign-refinement factors, and the accounting for failures. B2 separately gives a direct algorithm on bounded-type components.
