The proxy maps the original incidence graph to the goods-interaction graph. A4h1's one-good block solver applies there, and A4b gives the local solves for the bounded vertex-cover and few-margin extension. L4b then supplies the shared threshold and budget recovery for the game. A1 handles weak signs on a viability-preserving perturbation. This lifts the shared boundary ideas in B3d and A4h9 to mixed articulation trees without requiring certified ties. [Research direction](../DIRECTIONS.md#direction-compose-solvers) · [Proxy and exact recovery](../notes/A4h-buyer-proxy.md#replace-one-buyer-by-a-good-and-a-set-of-two-option-buyers) · [Block corollary](../notes/A4h-buyer-proxy.md#an-exact-solver-on-bounded-incidence-blocks)

The exact lift check used Python's rational arithmetic and seed 96391, with one to six goods and buyers. It generated a clearing certificate from chosen rational prices, row utilities, and supported trades. The proof gives an equation-by-equation reconstruction for any such certificate.

From the repository root, the following replay includes outside flow in the recovered original row.

```sh
python3 - <<'PY'
import sys
from fractions import Fraction as Q
sys.path.insert(0, "experiments/market_checks")
from a4h9_hybrid_examples import check_clearance, check_strict_hall

offers = {"row": {"a": (1, 1), "b": (1, 2)}}
caps = {"a": 1, "b": Q(1, 2)}
prices = {"a": 1, "b": Q(1, 2)}
old = {"row": {"a": 1, "b": Q(1, 2), None: Q(1, 2)}}
check_clearance(offers, {"row": 2}, caps, prices, old)
check_strict_hall(offers, {"row": 2}, caps, prices)

new_offers = {"a-row": {"proxy": (3, 1), "a": (2, 1)},
              "b-row": {"proxy": (3, 1), "b": (2, 2)},
              "guard": {"proxy": (2, 1)}}
new = {"a-row": {"proxy": 3, "a": 1},
       "b-row": {"proxy": Q(7, 2), "b": Q(1, 2)},
       "guard": {"proxy": Q(7, 2), None: Q(1, 2)}}
check_clearance(new_offers, dict.fromkeys(new, 4),
                {**caps, "proxy": 10}, {**prices, "proxy": 2}, new)
assert old["row"] == {"a": new["a-row"]["a"], "b": new["b-row"]["b"],
                      None: new["guard"][None]}
print("Proxy sales and recovered departures agree exactly.")
```
