Deleting $r$ coordinates to leave a slope-balanced interior gives a polynomial-size conditional convex quadratic program and an October-based bound of $2^{O(\sqrt r\log(2+L))}\mathrm{poly}(L)$. The earlier A4g sign test extends to guessing only in unbalanced biconnected blocks. Balanced separator recursion gives a $2^{O(d^{1/4}\log(2+L))}\mathrm{poly}(L)$ bound on planar incidence graphs. [Structural proofs and source dependencies](../notes/A4m-structural-elimination.md)

Independently, the November rational-circuit corollary gives a $2^{O(\tau)}\mathrm{poly}(L)$ exact solver using cut coordinates on a vertex cover of size $\tau$. The component LP variant applies the November approximation theorem to an evaluable sup-norm contraction over cuts of a supplied boundary. These use the full binary masses and capacities. [Reduced cut construction](../notes/A4m-structural-elimination.md#cut-coordinates-on-a-vertex-cover) · [Pinned November theorem and corollary](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Strongly-polynomial-linear-programming-November-24-2026/build/contractions.tex)

The structural replay checks a quadratic optimality certificate, 20 LCP complementarity pairs at an articulation, 512 physical cut comparisons, and 256 independent proposal pairs. It also reconstructs a known rational solution after a nonzero cut residual. [Exact structural replay](../experiments/a4m_structure_replay.py)
