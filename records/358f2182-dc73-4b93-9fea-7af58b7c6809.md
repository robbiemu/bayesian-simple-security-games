A4b's conditional price switch and strict Hall let a failing upper flow test at fixed port prices exclude a region after the private market is solved again. Merge the cut's ports into one auxiliary good to prove the projected cut and enlarge it with a rational price separation bound. A4h8 supplies the mixed-border context and A4h12 extends the cut to buyer quotes through its exact price proxy. The supplied two-buyer example rules out using the raw loads as convex subgradient data after a continuous one-to-one quote transformation.
