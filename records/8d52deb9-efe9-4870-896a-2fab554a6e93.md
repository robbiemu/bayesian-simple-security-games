The construction turns a counting-network cost curve into a transportation value along a proportional margin ray. A dedicated sink row makes the displacement from the original network margins small. A non-affinity in each witness band forces a landing of Algorithm 4, because every interior interpolation of an Algorithm 4 call is affine in the margins and is optimal at those margins. The open boundary is fixed across the witness range.

At depth $h$ this gives at least $2^h-1$ strict drops, with $2h+2$ targets, $O(h)$ types, unit losses, two defender gains and $O(h^3)$ bits. Strictly dominated targets can equalize the type and target counts. The lower bound works with any fixed seed order and exact optimizing flows. The [long fixed-flow path](../notes/B3f-forced-flow-switches.md) concerns attack changes at a fixed threshold.

Run `python3 -B experiments/l1a_threshold_replay.py 1 2 3 4`. It reports 3, 7, 15, and 31 strict drops with the same open boundary in the four fixtures. The replay uses a fixed seed order and one exact reflow policy. [Proof and construction](../notes/L1a-strict-threshold-drops.md)
