The [PR #8 review](https://github.com/robbiemu/bayesian-simple-security-games/pull/8#issuecomment-7089066389) formulates precision improvement as a route to exact recovery. Stable flows in finite quote lists give price boxes of width proportional to the mesh times a product bound. A4c supplies the strict capacity gap at the two endpoints, and the determinant repair follows the approach in A4d. The slope condition is preserved in L4b's supply and reserve calls. [Proof, exact recovery, and counterexamples](../notes/A4f-precision-scaling.md)

Run `python3 experiments/a4f_unit_tick_scaling.py` and `python3 experiments/a4f_contract_scaling_replay.py`. The unit replay checks 2,160 phases, and the fractional replay checks 1,400 phases. The latter uses small token expansions to generate reference allocations. The polynomial-time proof calls stable flow directly on the original rational quotas.
