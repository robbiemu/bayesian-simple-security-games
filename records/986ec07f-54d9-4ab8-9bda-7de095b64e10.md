A4h6 supplies the alternating-update structure. The unit clearing case studied in A4i1a permits integral matching and one free root per insertion. Minimum-product matchings leaving each reached buyer free give a supported rate vector. The strict improvement of one deletion-matching objective bounds internal events. The same argument keeps every retained price and rate at polynomial bit length. [Proof and reference solver](../notes/A4j-unit-matching-ascent.md)

Run `python3 experiments/a4j_unit_prices.py --self-test 2000`. It compares the matching costs with exhaustive search on 2,000 seeded rational markets and verifies the resulting Hall certificates. The run makes 4,727 updates, including 283 internal updates.
