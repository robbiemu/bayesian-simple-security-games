Scale the masses and capacities to integer counts in the rounded conservative circuit. At a fixed point its actual comparator transfers give an exactly feasible flow and complementary node balances. A small edge violation follows from rounding. One rational price-and-utility LP preserves the flow and repairs the original demand constraints, using a support gap over only the node variables and a tolerance. Integral quantities permit integral transfers, including matchings for unit quantities. The October lattice search retains its vertex-cover runtime, and A4c still selects the original minimum prices.
