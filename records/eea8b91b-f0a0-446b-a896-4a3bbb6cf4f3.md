The earlier ascent preserves selected tight deletion matchings through an internal event. Tassa's standard decomposition gives fixed components for each entire viable deletion graph. Because the surviving plans achieve their old component minima, the new minima can only decrease and one must decrease at each internal event. The resulting sum bound can be polynomial even when the product set and simple-cycle count are superpolynomial. The original square-chain control is credited in its own record.
