# Publication record

This is a public working edition of the investigation of Vincent Conitzer’s open problem. The initial commits assemble completed research from 21–22 November 2026. They group material by research question and carry their actual publication-preparation dates. The contribution catalog identifies who proposed a direction, which work followed, and which evidence supports that attribution. A Git author on an import commit identifies the publisher. [Original problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08796)

The mathematical notes are working arguments. Their terms “proved” and “checked” describe the work reported in those notes. The B3g audit was a separate model-assisted proof check. The Lean package checks individual library lemmas and examples. The general exact polynomial-time problem remains open in this investigation.

Third-party papers are cited at their sources. The supplied 34-page research collection has a bibliographic record and file digest. [Collection record](sources/prior-collection.md) · [Lean check coverage](lean/README.md)
