# A4h6 · Jump to new demand ties using alternating forests

**🟨 Partial result**

Alternating forests yield exact safe price jumps. Even a deficit of one can produce many internal events.

**Open task** — Bound a stronger shortcut on general inputs, or identify another class with polynomially many jumps.
