# A4i1 · Find the exact minimum prices of one supplied unit market

**🟦 Open**

Each buyer has unit mass and each good has unit capacity. Every offer can have its own positive rational slope.

**Open task** — Find a polynomial-bit-time solver with arbitrary affine slopes and recover a clearing assignment. The degree-three reduction gives a sufficient restricted problem.
