# A4i1 · Find the exact minimum prices of one supplied unit market

**🟦 Open**

Each buyer has unit mass and each good has unit capacity. Every offer can have its own positive rational slope.

**Open task** — Give a polynomial-bit-time algorithm for the minimum prices of a supplied unit market with arbitrary affine slopes, together with exact clearing-flow recovery.
