## Collection guide

Physical pages 1–2 give the contents and provenance of the three working drafts.

## Part I

Pages 3–9 address strictly positive rank-one protection losses, including the parametric LP and a counterexample. The review records an endpoint correction on pages 5–7.

## Part II

Pages 10–17 give the threshold ordering, the market correspondence, the rent-division embedding, and the two-good auction example.

## Part III

Pages 18–34 give the additive approximation scheme and exact fixed-type arrangement algorithm. The review points to the specific proof steps and assumptions in each case.
